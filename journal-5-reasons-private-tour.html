Why a private driver guided tour shows you so much more of Wales.

1. “You’ll just love where we take you!”

Enjoy touring without the crowds and at a pace that suits you. We’ll take you places where the big tour buses can’t reach or go. You’ll discover so much more on a private tour than you could ever do on your own, and best of all, it will feel like you have Wales all to yourself.

2. It’s your own exclusive tour

Enjoy door to door touring with a pick-up right from the front door of where you are staying here in North Wales. As it’s your very own exclusive tour, you choose the start and end times of the touring day, and we tour at a pace that suits you. You are in control of the itinerary, so you can amend and change the tour to suit what you want to do and see.

Our advantage is that your personal Tour Director guides you through all the sites and attractions that are part of your tour. All too often we see international visitors dropped off at a site and left to their own devices, whereas we bring the destination to life with great stories and interesting facts.

3. Your tour is guaranteed to operate

When you book with us, you can be confident in the delivery and operation of our tour services. As there are no other parties involved, it’s your very own private tour, and unlike many other operators, it’s not subject to minimum numbers to operate.

We are with you every step of the way, from your very first enquiry to operating the tour, and unlike some other driver guide operators, we never sub-contract or farm your booking out for someone else to operate.

4. We live in Wales and we know Wales

Many tour companies who feature Wales don’t know the country as well as we do, and many send their drivers to operate a tour in a land they don’t know. Anyone can take you down the main highways, but we’ll prove you made the right choice by the roads and places we take you. We love touring the back roads, along the old drover roads, and even pick up ancient tracks laid down by the Romans during their occupation of the region. We’ll introduce you to hidden gems and secret locations for those very special photographs.

5. A personal Tour Director and Tour Concierge

Probably the most important part of any tour, and a service that not many other driver guides offer: your personal Tour Director works and communicates directly with you from your very first enquiry, and is with you every step of the way, right through to operating the tour himself.

He is a professionally trained tour guide and has designed tours for some of the leading European and international tour operators, as well as cruise lines. Your Tour Director and Concierge will also save you the time and hassle of researching suitable accommodation and finding the best restaurants.

Our private tour services are always in big demand, so if you like what we offer, our best advice is not to let someone else beat you to our availability.
