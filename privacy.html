Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

Who we are

This website is run by Boutique Tours of Wales Ltd, company number 11905538. In this policy, “we”, “us” and “our” mean Boutique Tours of North Wales. We decide how the information you share with us is used, which makes us the “controller” of that information under the UK General Data Protection Regulation and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Our registered office address is on the public register at Companies House under company number 11905538. You can contact us about privacy using the enquiry form on our Contact page.

The short version

  • We don’t use analytics, advertising trackers or tracking cookies on this website.
  • We only receive personal information when you choose to contact us, through the enquiry form on this website or by replying to our emails.
  • We use it to reply to you and to provide what you asked for. We never sell it.

What we collect

When you contact us, we receive the details you choose to send, such as your name, email address, phone number and the content of your message. When you send the enquiry form, it is emailed to us through our website hosting provider, and we keep your enquiry in our email account so we can reply and arrange your tour.

Like every website, our hosting provider automatically records basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested. These server logs are used to keep the website secure and working, and are kept for a short period set by our host.

Why we use it, and our lawful basis

  • To reply to your enquiry and provide our services. This is necessary to take steps you ask for before entering into a contract, or to perform a contract with you.
  • To keep records of our work and meet legal duties, such as tax and accounting rules. This is a legal obligation.
  • To keep this website secure. This is our legitimate interest in running a safe website.

We won’t send you marketing messages unless you have asked us to, and you can ask us to stop at any time.

Cookies and tracking

This website doesn’t set tracking or advertising cookies, and it doesn’t use analytics. Your browser may store small technical preferences that never leave your device, such as whether you have seen our opening animation and the stops you add to your day book. The day book stays in your browser until you send it to us yourself. Because of this, we don’t need to show a cookie banner.

Services we rely on

Some information passes through trusted providers that help us run our business:

  • WhatsApp (Meta Platforms), when you message us on WhatsApp. Their own privacy policy applies to how WhatsApp handles your messages.
  • Our email provider, which hosts our inbox, when you email us or send an enquiry by email.
  • Our web hosting provider, which stores this website and its server logs.
  • YouTube (Google), only if you press play on the video on our About page. The video is not loaded until you do, and it uses YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode.
  • Booking.com and other accommodation websites, only if you follow a link from our Where to stay page. Their own privacy policies apply once you leave our website.
  • Google Fonts, which delivers the fonts this website uses. Your browser connects to Google’s servers to download them, which shares your IP address with Google.
  • The jQuery CDN, which delivers a script this website uses. Your browser connects to its servers to download the file, which shares your IP address with the CDN.

Some of these providers may process information outside the UK or European Economic Area. Where they do, they rely on recognised safeguards such as adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for as long as we need them to reply and to provide our services, and we hold booking records for a minimum of six years, after which you can ask us to delete them. Records we must keep by law, such as invoices, are kept for the period the law requires, usually six years in the UK.

Your rights

You have the right to ask for a copy of the information we hold about you, and to ask us to correct it, delete it, restrict how we use it, or transfer it to you or someone else. You can also object to how we use it. To use any of these rights, email the enquiry form on our Contact page. We’ll reply within one month.

If you’re unhappy with how we have handled your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Keeping it safe

We limit who can see your information and use reputable providers that protect it. No method of sending information over the internet is completely secure, so please don’t send sensitive details such as payment card numbers through WhatsApp, email or the contact form.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

All you have to do is ask and if it is possible, then it will be our pleasure to accommodate your wishes.
