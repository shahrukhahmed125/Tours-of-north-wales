Medieval Conwy, Bodnant Garden and Eryri (Snowdonia)

Wales is full of culture, history and scenery. This tour combines all three elements and provides you with a great overview of the region all in one day!

The tour features the UNESCO word heritage site with its castle and medieval walls at Conwy. Conwy was developed under the reign of King Edward 1st in the 13th century and is one of the mist picturesque towns to be found in Wales

Our tour follows the river into the Snowdonia National Park, where we stop to explore the world-famous historic Bodnant Gardens. The gardens are a spectacle no matter what time of the year you visit, with over 80 acres

Our tailor-made private tour itinerary can also take in further stops at other interesting sites, depending on your touring time that you choose for the day.

What’s on the Touring Route

The options below provide you with a choice of places to see and things to do during your touring day with us. We will work with you to design the perfect custom tailor made tour around your personal preferences and choices.

Please note: It is not possible to cover and include every location in one day. Some attractions have restricted operating and opening times.

Tap “Add to my day” on the places you would love to see. Your choices are kept in “My day” at the top of every page and sent with your enquiry.

The Medieval Walled Town of Conwy

Enjoy a walk around the medieval town walls of Conwy, claimed to be the best preserved in Europe. Stretching almost uninterrupted around the town, they run for three quarters of a mile and feature 21 towers and 3 original gateways.

Conwy Castle

There isn’t a better place in Britain to stand on the battlements of a castle. Conwy offers unrivalled views overlooking the estuary and having a landscape backdrop of the Snowdonia Hills. We think it’s one of the most magnificent medieval fortresses to be found anywhere in Europe, going back to 13th century. *Entrance fee applies.

Bodnant Gardens

Rated as one of the best gardens to visit in Great Britain. Bodnant Gardens spreads out across 80 acres of hillside and includes one the finest rose gardens in Wales, set amongst five formal Italianate terraces. *Entrance fee applies.

Llanrwst

The famous chocolate box photo stop with one of the most photographed buildings in Wales. The former 15th century medieval courthouse is situated on the banks of the river Conwy and nestles beside a 400 year old, three arched stone bridge, which crosses the river Conwy at Llanrwst.

Betws-y-Coed

A lovely Victorian Spa town, where you can discover a 14th century medieval church hidden in the woods, nestling beside a scenic river.

Swallow Falls

Enjoy one of the most scenic and stunning waterfalls in Wales. *Entrance Fee Payable

Glacial Valley of the Sheep Farms

We tour a beautiful glacial valley along a trail that was originally laid down by the Romans. There are several stops for photographs and even a chance to take a short walk amongst the most magnificent scenery of the Snowdonia National Park.

The Conwy, Bodnant Gardens and Snowdonia Tour can be custom designed to fit a number of touring options such as duration and pick up/drop off locations.

This private tour can start from Beaumaris, Bangor, Betws-y-Coed, Caernarfon, Conwy, Holyhead, Llandudno, Ruthin, Llangollen and Wrexham areas.

If you are staying anywhere else in the North Wales region, then please contact us to discuss your tour requirements.

The tour can also be taken as part of a transfer tour to/from Chester, Liverpool, Manchester, Holyhead and your accommodation in North Wales.

This tour can also be customised to operate from both Holyhead Port and Liverpool Cruise Terminal as an exclusive Private Tour for cruise ship passengers. We will create an itinerary around the time that you have in port.
